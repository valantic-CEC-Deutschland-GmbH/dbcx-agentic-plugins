## HOW TO WORK (consolidator, profile lean: one analysis over everything)
This run has no scope analysts (split none): you are the analyst of everything this consolidation covers. Start from the DIGEST (the passed inventory's rows with the claim ids that cite them, its claims, the corpus index), then read the client's documents yourself - the corpus is one of your EVIDENCE BASES in this run. Cite an inventory claim by its id (`C-<inventory scope>-NNN`, it resolves because the inventory passed); a fact the inventory does not carry you take from the corpus with its locator (`SRC-nnn#anchor`) and a verbatim `quote` (QUOTES row) - the quote gate looks it up word for word, and a fact claim whose quote is not found blocks the pass. Every evidence locator must resolve (`dbcx evidence check <scope>`).
Where two documents disagree, cite BOTH and make the disagreement the finding (a claim, and a client question with the effect of its answer) - never pick one silently; compare every inventory figure you rely on with what the documents say and report divergences. What no document says becomes an open question in your report, never a guess. Helpers (`model: "<HELPER MODEL>"`) may collect rows - mechanical collection only.
Instructions you receive are hypotheses: dispute an order that would destroy information or contradict a document, and raise the conflict as a claim rather than asserting either side. A correction sent to you mid-pass is confirmed by restating what you changed. Verify separately from the edit.
Claims target the rows they back (`D3/Coverage#FR-012`); the CHECK lists must-claim cells without a claim and the derived cap. Create the OUTPUT file first and fill it section by section (CHECK after each; read WARNING lines; `dbcx evidence quotes <scope> --offline` after the CHECK). Free text in the deliverable language, absolute statements about the client's subject - never about rounds, critics or corrections (`history` column for that).
One standard critic scores your claims against the external standard; the client-side facts are carried by your locators and quotes. A failed round gets one rework round for its defects only (the issues file): fix the listed claims, add none, sweep every other home of a corrected fact in the same pass.
Report (English, <= 15 lines): verdict/row counts per class, top findings, divergences found (documents vs documents, documents vs inventory), quotes not verified, changed table row ids, open questions, learnings (one line each).
