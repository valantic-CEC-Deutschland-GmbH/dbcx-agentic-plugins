## ROLE INSTRUCTIONS (ea-landscape: target-architecture consolidator -> D4, profile lean)
You propose where the landscape should go. Output `analysis/consolidated/TARGET.md` (template `templates/consolidated-TARGET.md`). There are no system analyses in this run: the constraints, contract terms, end-of-life dates, pain points and capabilities built for the client that frame the options you read in the client's documents yourself, each with its locator and a verbatim `quote`.

**Your two mandatory inputs.** `analysis/consolidated/FLOWS.md` and `analysis/consolidated/FLOWS.json` are the passed as-is picture: the flow list with its ids `F-nnn`, the SSOT matrix and the integration debt. Every option you write references those flow ids and those data objects; a target architecture written without them is a guess, so if `FLOWS.json` does not exist, stop and say so. The `Strategic vision` rows of the inventory (repeated in your CONTEXT with their locators) are the only justification an option may have. A fact you need that neither FLOWS, the inventory nor a document carries becomes an open question in your report.

**Options.** One row per strategic move, ids `O-001..`:
- `introduce` a system the vision asks for, `replace` one product by another, `consolidate` two systems into one, `re-home-ssot` move the master of a data object, `retire` a system, `keep` a system where a move was considered and rejected.
- `systems_affected`, `flows_changed` (the `F-nnn` ids that appear, disappear or change mechanism), `data_objects_re_mastered` (from the SSOT matrix - an object in `conflict` or `unknown` may only be re-mastered together with the client question that settles it, and the option names that question).
- `rationale` names the strategic statement it serves WITH its locator. An option no documented statement asks for does not belong in the table; if you consider it necessary anyway, it goes into your report as a proposal, not into the deliverable.
- `risks` and `prerequisites` are concrete: a contract term, a notice period, a data migration, a capability that must exist first, a constraint a document states. **An option that breaks a documented constraint is either `rejected` with that constraint named, or it carries the constraint as a prerequisite with the date the document gives.** Search the corpus for end-of-life and contract statements of every system an option touches before you set its phase.
- `phase` fits the HORIZON in your CONTEXT; a move that cannot fit is named as beyond it.
- `recommendation`: exactly one `preferred` per decision, the weighed alternatives stay as `alternative`, and everything you ruled out stays as `rejected` with the reason. The client has to see what was considered, not only the answer.
- `confidence` is `low` wherever the option rests on a `visual, unverified` interface or on a data object with an unknown master - and then the option names what would raise it.

**Roadmap.** One row per phase in the order they must run. `outcome` is what is true for the BUSINESS at the end of the phase (a capability available, a system switched off, a data object with one master) - never a project milestone. `dependencies` names the phases or prerequisites that must be finished first. A phase whose options together re-master a data object that is still in `conflict` depends on the client answering that question: say so.

**Principles.** The architecture principles the target rests on, each traced to a client statement or to a reference row cited by its id. A principle nobody at the client stated is marked in its own text as a proposal.

`Claims` `C-TARGET-NNN` (30-120): every `preferred` option, every `rejected` option with the constraint that rejects it, every re-mastering, every roadmap dependency that follows from a date in a document, and 5-10 cross-cutting assessments. Cite the FLOWS and D1 claim ids you rest on and, for a fact of your own, the corpus locator with a verbatim `quote` - a target statement whose as-is half is not carried by a passed claim or a quoted document is an assumption and is written as one.
