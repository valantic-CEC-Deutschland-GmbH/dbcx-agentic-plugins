# mach-composable pack notes

Provenance: hand-written on 2026-09-08 for the dbcx-agentic-loop plugin. No vendor or product document was converted — the content is generalised delivery practice from composable and MACH programmes, including ones that were reversed, with all client-specific material removed. Nothing in it is a verified external fact, which is why the reference-cache starts empty.

- **Deliberate asymmetry**: the file is roughly a third of the length of `cx-reference/reference-architecture.md`, and the longest section is "When not to go composable". That is the section a vendor deck omits and the one an assessment needs; keep it the centre of gravity when extending.
- **Written by hand, not derived**: the four principles restated as client obligations, the five patterns, the seven decline reasons, the operating-model consequences and the checklist. The register follows `knowledge/platforms/sap-commerce/pack.json`, read as the style reference and not modified.
- **Kind and roles**: `kind: "reference-architecture"` (singular) inside `knowledge/reference-architectures/` (plural), `roles: ["reference_architecture"]`, snippets at the top level — non-platform packs are read from the top-level `snippets` map by the engine.
- **`docs_domains` is empty on purpose**: MACH and composable are architectural claims, not products. There is no official documentation domain, and naming a vendor or an industry-body page would invite a critic to cite marketing material as evidence.
- **No `adapter.py`, no `knowledge.json`**: the pack scans nothing and holds no target-version fact lists. File set per the contract: pack.json, reference-architecture.md, research-seed.md, reference-cache.md, NOTES.md.
- **Relationship to `cx-reference`**: this pack takes the platform question, `cx-reference` takes the landscape and data-ownership question. They overlap on one point by design — that a data-ownership problem is not solved by a platform change — because that is the most common reason a composable programme fails to deliver its business case.
- **What a researcher must resolve first**: the platform team's run capacity, whether in-house frontend and integration capability exist, whether an integration platform and an interface inventory with volumes already exist, and the cost shape (capital versus ongoing engineering). Without those four, sections 3 to 5 cannot be answered and no composable recommendation is defensible.
- **How to extend**: new patterns need a "fits when" and a "does not fit when", never only the former. New decline reasons must be organisational or economic and observable in a workshop, not stylistic preference. Statements about a specific vendor's cadence, pricing metric or deprecation policy do not belong in this file — they are dated facts for reference-cache.md or for that vendor's platform pack.
