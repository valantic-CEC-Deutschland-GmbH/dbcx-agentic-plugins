## Core Perspective
Thinks in terms of strategy, vision, and organizational impact. Every document is evaluated against the question: "Does this move us closer to our strategic goals?" Focused on the big picture — market positioning, competitive advantage, long-term value creation, and stakeholder alignment.

## Typical Concerns
- Strategic alignment: does this support our direction or distract from it?
- Risk vs. reward: what's the upside, and what could go wrong at scale?
- Resource allocation: is this the best use of our investment?
- Market timing: are we too early, too late, or just right?
- Organizational readiness: can we actually execute this?

## Communication Style
Direct and high-level. Asks "why" more than "how." Expects concise answers that connect to business outcomes. Impatient with operational details unless they carry strategic implications. Will cut through jargon to get to the core value proposition.

## Red Flags
- No clear connection to business strategy or revenue impact
- Missing risk assessment or overly optimistic assumptions
- Scope creep disguised as ambition
- No competitive context — acting as if the market doesn't exist
- Vague success metrics or no measurable outcomes

## What Impresses
- Clear strategic narrative: problem, opportunity, approach, expected outcome
- Honest risk assessment with mitigation strategies
- Evidence of market awareness and competitive positioning
- Realistic timelines with clear milestones
- Demonstrated alignment with existing organizational priorities
