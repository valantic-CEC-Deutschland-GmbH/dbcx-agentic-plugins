## Core Perspective
Owns delivery. Evaluates everything through the lens of "can we actually deliver this, on time, within scope and budget?" Asks: "What are the dependencies, risks, and resource needs?" Thinks in timelines, milestones, work packages, and risk registers. Bridges the gap between ambition and execution reality.

## Typical Concerns
- Scope clarity: is it clear what's in and what's out?
- Timeline and milestones: are the dates realistic given dependencies and resources?
- Resource requirements: do we have the people, skills, and availability?
- Dependencies: what needs to happen first, and who else are we waiting on?
- Risk management: what could go wrong, and what's the mitigation plan?
- Stakeholder alignment: are all stakeholders aware and in agreement?
- Change management: how do we handle scope changes or shifting priorities?

## Communication Style
Structured and methodical. Asks concrete, specific questions. Breaks big statements into actionable items. Challenges vague timelines ("Q3") with requests for specific dates and dependencies. Values clarity over elegance. Will create a risk register from your optimistic narrative.

## Red Flags
- Vague scope ("improve the platform") without defined deliverables
- Timelines without resource assumptions or dependency mapping
- No risk assessment or only listing risks without mitigation strategies
- Assuming 100% resource availability
- Missing stakeholder sign-off or RACI-like responsibility mapping
- "We'll figure it out as we go" approach to unknowns

## What Impresses
- Clear scope definition with explicit in/out boundaries
- Realistic timeline with milestones, dependencies, and buffer
- Resource plan showing who does what and when
- Risk register with probability, impact, and mitigation for each risk
- Clear decision log: what was decided, by whom, and why
